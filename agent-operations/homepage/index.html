What it does

From first ring to five-star review.

Here's what your digital employee actually does on every lead — the whole loop, and everything in between.

  1. 01

    Answers the first ring

    Picks up every call and web lead in seconds, 24/7. If your office team is on the other line, it catches the overflow instead of letting voicemail take the job.

  2. 02

    Runs the intake

    Asks the questions you would ask: the property, the problem, the urgency, and how they found you.

  3. 03

    Creates the customer

    Enters the lead in FieldRoutes, ServiceTitan, or the system you already run. No duplicates, no lost slips of paper.

  4. 04

    Prices and sends the estimate

    Works from your price book and gets the estimate out while the customer is still on the hook.

  5. 05

    Books the job

    Puts the inspection or service on your schedule and confirms it with the customer.

  6. 06

    Gets the agreement signed

    Sends the service agreement and follows up until the signature is back — not filed away half-done.

  7. 07

    Follows up and collects the review

    Checks that the job went right, then asks for the five-star review while the customer is happiest.
