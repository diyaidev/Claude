# LSA → Google Ads (Performance Max) Migration: Client Explainer

Status: DRAFT for Ryan's review. Not sent anywhere. Built from public Google/trade-press sources on 2026-07-30 — verify against Ryan's own Google Ads account notices before this goes to any client, since Google's official in-product wording may differ slightly from press coverage.

## Why this exists

Google announced on July 19, 2026 that Local Services Ads (LSA) is folding into the main Google Ads platform, running through a new Performance Max campaign type built for pay-per-lead goals. The first migration wave starts in **early August 2026** and explicitly includes home-service categories — plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliance repair, house cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control, and moving — which is the core of who LSA Command manages campaigns for. Agency and attorney clients are going to start seeing Google's own migration notices in their accounts within days, and they will have questions before they have context. Getting ahead of that with a plain-language explainer is a trust play, not busywork — it's the difference between "my agency already knew about this and has a plan" and "wait, what is Google doing to my account now."

## What's actually changing

- The standalone Local Services Ads dashboard is going away. Campaigns move into Google Ads proper, as a Performance Max campaign type built specifically for pay-per-lead.
- Google Business Profile syncs to the campaign in real time — profile edits (hours, categories, service areas) flow through automatically instead of needing a separate LSA-side update.
- Rollout is phased: select US home/storefront service categories first (early August 2026), broader US categories later in 2026, non-US accounts and remaining categories in 2027.
- Account admins get a heads-up 14 days before their account migrates, a reminder 7 days after that, and a confirmation once migration completes. So a client account could see its first notice any day now if it's in the first wave.

## What's NOT changing (lead with this — it's the reassurance)

- Still pay-per-lead, not pay-per-click. You pay for calls, messages, and bookings — not impressions or clicks.
- Ads still only show on Google Search and Google Maps — this is not Performance Max's usual all-inventory placement behavior.
- Campaigns stay keywordless and still run off the Google Business Profile, the same way LSA always has.
- The "Google Guaranteed" / "Google Screened" badge and review-driven ranking mechanics are not described as changing in any of the source coverage — flag this as unconfirmed rather than promising it, since Google hasn't published exhaustive scope docs at time of writing.

## Client-facing FAQ (draft language — edit before sending)

**"Why is my dashboard different?"**
Google moved Local Services Ads into the main Google Ads platform. It's a Google-side change, not something we did to your account. Your leads, pricing model, and where your ad shows up don't change.

**"Am I going to pay more?"**
No indication of that in anything Google has published. Still pay-per-lead, same as before.

**"Do I need to do anything?"**
No action needed on your end — we're handling account-side items as each account gets its migration window. If Google's own 14-day notice lands in your inbox, that's expected; forward it to us so we can confirm the timeline for your account, we don't need you to act on it yourself.

**"When does this happen to me?"**
Depends on your category. Home services (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliance repair, cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control, moving) are in the first wave starting early August 2026. Other categories follow later this year into 2027.
