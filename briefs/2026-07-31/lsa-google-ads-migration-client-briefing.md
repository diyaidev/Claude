# LSA Command Client Briefing: The Google Ads Migration
*Draft for Ryan — review before sending. Not sent anywhere yet.*

**Why this exists:** Google announced on July 19, 2026 that Local Services Ads is folding into the main Google Ads platform. The standalone LSA dashboard goes away; pay-per-lead campaigns migrate into a new Performance Max campaign type built for pay-per-lead. Rollout starts August 2026 with a limited group of home/storefront categories (pest control, appliance repair) and expands through late 2026, reaching remaining categories and non-US accounts in 2027. Separately, Google's Local Services Ads requirements (formerly "Local Services platform policies") update took effect today, July 31, 2026, trimming outdated rules, and GBP's chat feature is being fully discontinued today as well.

For agency owners and law firms running LSA through LSA Command, this is the biggest structural change to the product since Google Guaranteed became Google Verified. Clients will start seeing dashboard changes, possibly with no warning from Google directly. The agency that explains it first — instead of the agency whose client found out from a Google support email — keeps the trust.

**Who should get this:** LSA Command agency clients currently running LSA for home-service or legal categories likely to be in the first migration wave.

## Draft email — "What's changing with your Local Services Ads (and what isn't)"

Subject: Heads up — Google is moving Local Services Ads into Google Ads

Hey [Name],

Quick heads-up on something coming from Google, not from us — wanted you to hear it here first instead of in a random Google email.

**What's happening:** Google is folding Local Services Ads into the main Google Ads platform. Instead of managing your LSA campaigns in the separate LSA dashboard, they'll live inside Google Ads as a new "Performance Max for pay-per-lead" campaign type.

**What's NOT changing:**
- You still only pay for valid leads — calls and messages — not clicks.
- Your ads still show up keywordless, Search + Maps only, same as today.
- Your Google Business Profile still drives the campaign — profile edits (hours, photos, services) still auto-update your ad.

**What IS changing:**
- The dashboard you're used to logging into goes away over time. Everything moves into Google Ads.
- Rollout is staged — Google's starting in August 2026 with a small set of categories (pest control, appliance repair first), then expanding through the rest of 2026 and into 2027 for everyone else. We don't yet have your specific migration date — Google hasn't published a category-by-category schedule.
- Reporting and lead management will look different once you're moved. We'll walk you through the new layout the week you migrate, not before — no point learning a screen that might still change.

**What we're doing on our end:** We're tracking Google's rollout schedule and will reach out the moment we see your account is scheduled to move, so nothing changes on your end without us telling you first.

Nothing to do right now. Just didn't want this to be a surprise.

—Ryan
