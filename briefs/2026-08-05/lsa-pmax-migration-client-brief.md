# LSA → Performance Max migration — client/partner communication + internal checklist

Drafted 2026-08-05. Not sent. Ryan/Alex to review, fill in bracketed specifics, and decide who actually receives it.

## Why this exists

Google announced (July 20, 2026) that Local Services Ads is folding into Google Ads as a
specialized Performance Max campaign type. Phase 1 rollout starts **this month** for a first
wave of U.S. home-service categories: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliance repair, house
cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control, and moving — plus pet care, wellness, and education
in the earliest cohort. Non-storefront service-area businesses and custom-bidding accounts
follow in "late 2026"; non-U.S. accounts in 2027.

This lands squarely on LSA Command's client base. Several open items right now (Heavy Lifting,
Search Prestige, Direct Phone Call Marketing's four clients — see Nate/Sky Media Ventures
dossier) sit in exactly these categories. If Google's automated migration notice reaches a
client's Google Ads admin before LSA Command has explained it, the client's first data point is
Google's generic email — not Ryan's team. Better to get ahead of it.

Confirmed mechanics (Google Ads Help, Search Engine Land, ppc.land, WordStream — all
July–August 2026):
- Account admins get a **14-day** notice, then a **7-day** reminder, before migration; Google
  confirms when it's done.
- Existing budgets, settings, and creative transfer automatically. Where/how ads appear on
  Search and Maps is unchanged.
- The **standalone LSA dashboard goes away** after migration — everything moves into Google
  Ads.
- **Historical performance reports do NOT migrate.** Anything client-facing that references
  LSA-dashboard history should be exported/archived before their account migrates.
- Separately (not the same rollout, but worth flagging to any NY or EU-based client): as of
  **August 2, 2026**, Local Services Ads no longer allows AI-generated or AI-edited images in
  the EU and New York state.

## Draft client/partner email

Subject: Heads up — Google is folding Local Services Ads into Google Ads (here's what changes for you)

---

Hey [First Name],

Wanted to flag something before you hear about it from a generic Google email instead of us.

Google is retiring the standalone Local Services Ads dashboard and moving LSA campaigns into
regular Google Ads, run as a special Performance Max campaign type. It's rolling out in phases —
first wave is home-service categories like plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, pest control,
cleaning, and a few others, starting this month. [If client is in a first-wave category: "That
includes you."] [If not: "You're not in the first wave, but it's worth knowing what's coming."]

What actually changes for you:
- Where your ads show up on Search and Maps — nothing. Same placement, same pay-per-lead model.
- Where you manage things — everything moves into the regular Google Ads interface. The old LSA
  dashboard goes away once your account migrates.
- Your budgets, settings, and current campaign setup carry over automatically. We're not
  rebuilding anything from scratch.
- One real gap: historical performance reports don't carry over. We're pulling and archiving
  your report history now so you don't lose the record of what's worked.

Google gives a 14-day heads-up before your account actually migrates, then a 7-day reminder.
When that notice lands in your Google Ads admin inbox, that's Google confirming it's really
happening for your account — not spam, not a phishing attempt.

Nothing you need to do right now. We're tracking this on our end and will flag you the moment
your account gets its migration notice. If you want to hop on a call to walk through it, just
say the word.

[If NY-based client, add:] One more thing specific to you: as of August 2, Google no longer
allows AI-generated or AI-edited images in Local Services Ads for New York accounts. If any of
your current ad photos were AI-touched, let's swap them before this becomes an issue.

Talk soon,
Ryan
