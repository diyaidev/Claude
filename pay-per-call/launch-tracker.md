## The deal in one paragraph

Ryan recruits and vets "call sellers" — lead-gen operators with assets that ring (GBPs,
rank-and-rent sites, Google Ads, LSA accounts) — and routes their qualified home-service calls
into Dalton's Zion pay-per-call network as a sub-affiliate under Daniel Foster. Revenue is
per-qualified-call payouts; Daniel's cut is 25% of Dalton's margin on Ryan's accounts (roughly
5–20% of call value). New sellers run a probation period on more forgiving buyers at lower
payouts before going fully live.

## Economics (from the Jul 21 call with Daniel)

| Tier | Payout per call |
|---|---|
| Average qualified call | $35–$75 |
| High end | $100+ (occasional $400) |
| Technically qualified, low quality (short, no address, odd questions) | $0.70–$5 |
| Zip not on the buyer matrix ("connectivity") | $0 |

Qualified call = new caller, correct category, in service area, 60+ seconds.
